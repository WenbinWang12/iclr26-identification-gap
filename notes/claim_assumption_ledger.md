## Prohibited wording before evidence

- “Forgetting is fundamentally/entirely a capacity problem.”
- “Sequential training necessarily causes monotone forgetting.”
- “Unused LoRA columns are interference-free.”
- “Top-k allocation is globally optimal for LoRA training.”
- “The method solves model merging.”
- “Full fine-tuning is the \(R\to\infty\) limit and has no capacity problem,”
  or that \(d_t\) measures output-function equivalence.
- “Reservoir replay (always/uniformly) loses the rare task,” or “\(d_t\)
  predicts retention” (it is computed from the same occupied subspace, so it is
  a consistency check, not an independent predictor), or that the arms are
  matched on memory/compute (only occupied rank is matched).
- “State of the art,” “significant,” or “consistent” without matched experiments
  and uncertainty intervals.
- “Full FCRA beats reservoir on every seed” (it is $\ge$ reservoir on $39/40$, not
  uniform), or that consolidation / the trust region fire on the orthogonal
  heavy-tail stream (they are deliberate no-ops there; the mechanism-stress stream
  is where they fire and it carries no retention claim), or that protection uses
  “historical curvature energy” (it uses the damped curvature-aware score
  $\bz^\top(\bA+\lambda\bI)^{-1}\bz$).
- “FCRA reduces forgetting overall / improves average retention,” or any POSITIVE
  D2-E method claim. The settled oracle-free result (C16) is an HONEST NEGATIVE: no
  online allocator we built reliably beats risk-aware (CVaR) replay on the tail
  ($11/40$ vs the $28/40$ bar). Prohibited: “FCRA recovers the rare task on the
  factored proxy” (the $+1.00$ headline was an oracle-init ARTIFACT, collapsing to
  $-0.04$ oracle-free); “the online allocator reaches/beats the Pareto frontier”
  (soft-protect is CLOSEST, dist $0.42$, but that is not beating risk-aware replay
  on the tail); “consolidation is shown to help in D2-E” (it is a byte-identical
  no-op on every stream). Permitted D2-E claims: the mean–tail Pareto frontier is
  the correct object under a rank floor; protection must be SOFT — hard-freezing
  diverges (worst $-2.03$) and soft-anchoring reaches the frontier (worst $-0.40$).
- **RETRACTED wording (2026-08-26 audit), must not reappear:** “FCRA's auxiliary
  memory is smaller than one replay window,” “the memory confound runs in FCRA's
  favor,” or any statement that D2-E's FCRA holds LESS memory than the reservoir —
  it holds the SAME 262 KB buffer. Also prohibited until the oracle-free rerun:
  “D2-E is a real / task-free / held-out-loss LoRA result” — the frozen run uses a
  direction ORACLE (`v=V[:,k]` for residual/score/atom-init) and an ANALYTIC
  retention metric, so it tests neither oracle-free discovery nor held-out loss.
  D2-E must be called a “factored low-rank proxy, under revision,” not a real-LoRA
  result. “FCRA beats baselines on the orthogonal stream” is fine only paired with
  the note that that stream is zero-sum and the honest object is a mean–tail Pareto
  frontier.
- **PSR-LoRA (C17) — bounds that must not be overstated.** Prohibited: “PSR-LoRA
  uniformly reduces forgetting,” “PSR-LoRA dominates every baseline,” or “PSR-LoRA
  beats risk-aware replay on every world” — the tail gain over excess-CVaR is a
  PAIRED-AVERAGE effect (wins $19/20$ worlds vs excess-CVaR, $20/20$ vs the uniform
  solve, on the held-out set; cluster CI (b) $[+0.070,+0.287]$), not uniform
  dominance; state the per-world split whenever the tail win is claimed.
  Prohibited: “the coverage buffer is confirmed to work in isolation” or citing the
  coverage increment as significant — the direct coverage ablation ($+0.0918$ vs
  the single reservoir) has cluster CI $[-0.0085,+0.1922]$ that STRADDLES 0;
  coverage is a component of a super-additive combination, reported as the mechanism
  that flips (b), never as an independently significant increment. Prohibited:
  citing the 2027-burned-seed cross-check as headline or as evidence stronger than
  the held-out test — it is POST-HOC (those seeds motivated the remedy), not
  draw-controlled confirmation; the ONLY independent confirmation is the held-out
  (2028xxxx) PASS. Prohibited: claiming criterion (c) is robust — it is
  DRAW-SENSITIVE (passes on the held-out draw, fails on the burned draw). Prohibited:
  “allocation manufactures capacity” or “PSR-LoRA removes the spectral-tail floor” —
  it REDISTRIBUTES the fixed budget to lift worst-group retention ABOVE the floor at
  matched mean; the floor itself is unremovable (Theorem 1). Prohibited: “PSR-LoRA
  is certified safe on held-out data” — the never-worse-than-uniform guarantee is on
  the BUFFERED worst group only, not a held-out certificate. Prohibited: any
  “real-LoRA / LLM / benchmark” framing — C17 is a controlled LINEAR factored
  proxy and D2-B (a two-layer ReLU MLP, NOT a Transformer) shows the curvature-tail
  prediction does not yet transfer to the nonlinear regime. “PSR-LoRA redistributes
  the budget to raise worst-group retention” is correct only when paired with “in
  the linear regime where the floor is exactly characterized.” The word
  “pre-registered” is downgraded to “internally pre-specified, hash-recorded
  held-out evaluation”; the pre-specification lives in code
  (`preregister_heldout.py` + `psr_lora.py` DEFAULT, source-hashed before the run)
  and is transcribed post-hoc in `notes/phase2f_psr_lora_protocol.md`. The stale
  bounds “$12/20$,” “CI lower bound $+0.006$,” “tail $0.678$,” and “mean
  $0.971\pm0.009$” belong to the SUPERSEDED uniform-16 record
  (`outputs_compare_v2.txt`) and must not be cited as the method's result.
